The new staff app: the layout on a laptop

The look is Paper (your pick, 13 Nov). The choice now is how the app uses a laptop's width. Open each on a laptop; on a phone all three are the same single column. Samples with made-up bills.

  • 1 · Side by sideYour steps on the left, your bills on the right, all on one screen. A step has its letter on the left and the hearing beside it.›
  • 2 · WorkspaceA menu down the left, your steps and bills in a list, and whatever you pick opens beside it. You never lose your place.›
  • 3 · One columnWhat you saw first: everything down the middle, one page at a time.›

The three looks, from before

Colour and type only; Paper was chosen and the others can be swapped in later.

  • A · PaperQuiet and editorial. Lines and space, almost no boxes. Public mode turns the page deep plum.›
  • B · LagoonSoft and welcoming. Rounded cards on a cool mist. Public mode turns warm sunset orange.›
  • C · SlateCrisp and exact, like a fine tool. White with thin lines. Public mode puts a violet frame round the page.›

What to try in each

  1. Home: is it clear what needs you, and where your bills stand?
  2. Tap "Write testimony", then "Send for approval".
  3. Open HB 1563: is everything you would look up at the Capitol there?
  4. At the foot of the bill, "Change what the public sees about this bill": does it feel like a different place?
  5. Explore, and "See a calm day" on Home.
