HIPHI Bill Tracker

Text alerts: terms

What you get

Texts from the HIPHI Bill Tracker, run by the Hawaiʻi Public Health Institute: when a bill on the issues you follow gets a hearing, and when HIPHI asks people to speak up on them. At most one text a day.

How you signed up

You typed your number in the tracker and confirmed it with the 6-digit code we texted you.

Cost

The texts are free from us. Message and data rates from your phone company may apply.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any text (or QUIT, END, CANCEL or UNSUBSCRIBE). We send one last text to say you’re stopped. You can also turn texts off under Alerts in your profile, or email contact@hiphi.org and we’ll stop them within 10 business days.

Help

Reply HELP, or email contact@hiphi.org.

Your number

We use it only to send these texts and, if you choose, to sign you in. We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. More in our privacy page.

Delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
