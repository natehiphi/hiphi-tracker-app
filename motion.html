Motion

Every moment that moves, side by side, each playing in its own copy of the practice tracker. Watch at full speed, slow it to a quarter, or turn on Reduce Motion to see what people with that setting get. Name a moment by the name under it.

Each moment plays once when it comes into view. Changing the speed or Reduce Motion plays them again.

Each frame is the practice tracker on a phone (390 by 760), frozen on Mon 16 March 2026. Nothing here is saved, counted or sent: every frame keeps its own storage and asks the page not to track. The times are measured from the tap to the last thing that moves, in real-time seconds even when slowed. The rules these follow are DESIGN.md A-10 (motion) and C-7 (celebration).
